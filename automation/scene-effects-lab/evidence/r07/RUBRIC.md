# Scene judge rubric v1

Pass threshold: **95/100**. Weights were chosen by the user on 2026-11-07 and must
remain fixed across revision rounds. Rendering without an exception is not a quality score.

Judge only the supplied rendered evidence, never implementation effort or claims.
Review the timeline, large settled frame, portrait composition and light-background
frame. Static frames do not establish temporal smoothness: use the separately reported
frame-difference metrics as limited supporting evidence, not as a substitute for judgment.

Score each category 0–100:

| Category | Weight | What earns a high score |
|---|---:|---|
| Spectacle | 40% | A striking visual event and memorable payoff; powerful scale, depth, transformation or a precise reveal. Minimalism must still have impact. |
| Readability | 20% | The sung words are legible, hierarchy is clear, no unintended clipping or collisions, adequate contrast in both orientations and backgrounds. |
| Choreography | 20% | Anticipation, development and a purposeful finish; coherent timing, transitions and word-to-word handoffs. No arbitrary spinning or distracting decoration. |
| Craft | 15% | Professional spacing, type selection, composition, color relationships and finish. Looks designed, not an assortment of presets. |
| Originality | 5% | A distinctive combination of decisions, without an obvious one-to-one recreation of a known work. |

Calibration: 20 = rudimentary; 50 = rough demonstration; 70 = usable but ordinary;
85 = polished professional execution; 95 = exceptional, compelling and essentially
free of visible weaknesses. A plain fade is not spectacular. Flashiness alone does
not compensate for illegibility. Do not increase scores merely because this is a revision.

The recorded score is the weighted sum. Any critical defect caps the passing score
at 94 until corrected. Report concrete evidence and actionable corrections. Do not
assume unmeasured consistency or uniqueness equals 100. Scores are model judgments,
not objective facts or a guarantee of audience response.
