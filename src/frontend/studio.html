Review
Before any illustration is painted, the vision model checks each new reference picture: only its subject (no other person, such as the lead, drawn into it), the look its description gives, and the project's art style. One that fails is painted and checked again, up to ten rounds, before the illustrations start. Needs a vision model (Models & GPU).

Reading flow
How the words arrive on screen. Each flow uses a different set of effects: the line under the menu names them, and the Word effects and Kinetic tabs say the same from their side.
Real-time mixes typewriter, heavy and huge hero words, follows the newest word with the camera and reacts to the melody.
The AI reads each block of lyrics and decides which word gets a picture, what it shows, how it moves, and whether it replaces the word's letter O. Particle effects tagged “top only”, such as maple trees, grow on each block's top row; the other rows stay plain with one red accent word.
Scene effects
The AI picks one ticked scene for each verse, chorus and bridge, matching it to the lyrics. Each card explains its entry and exit behavior. Configure a scene to adjust its supported motion, color and layout controls; style filters keep your selections.
